**Ramayana: Part 1 reached an estimated ₹1,777 crore worldwide in 22 days**, leaving it **₹23 crore short of ₹1,800 crore**. The other headline came from the makers, who say the film is now the **highest-grossing Indian production in 27 countries**. Those headlines measure different things. One adds ticket sales around the world, while the other compares the film with previous Indian releases in individual markets. [Sacnilk](https://www.sacnilk.com/news/ramayana_part_1_2026_Box_Office_Collection_Day_Wise_Worldwide) · [Moneycontrol](https://www.moneycontrol.com/entertainment/ranbir-kapoor-s-film-becomes-highest-grossing-indian-production-in-27-countries-collects-rs-1800-crore-worldwide-article-14090302.html)

## Ramayana box office collection after 22 days

| Measure | Estimated collection |
| --- | ---: |
| Day 22 India net, all languages | ₹9.65 crore |
| Total India net | ₹992.10 crore |
| Total India gross | ₹1,185 crore |
| Overseas gross | ₹592 crore |
| **Worldwide gross** | **₹1,777 crore** |

Worldwide gross adds **India gross and overseas gross**. India net excludes ticket taxes and is a separate measure. Overseas accounted for about **33.3%** of worldwide gross. These are trade estimates through **26 November 2026**, the film’s 22nd day, and can be revised. [Sacnilk Day 22 data](https://www.sacnilk.com/news/ramayana_part_1_2026_Box_Office_Collection_Day_Wise_Worldwide)

## What ‘No. 1 in 27 countries’ covers

The makers’ claim compares *Ramayana* with other **Indian productions** in the markets they named. Reported examples include the **US, Canada, UK, Thailand, Indonesia, Nepal, the Netherlands, Vietnam, South Africa and Kenya**. It is a cumulative earnings claim within that group. A ranking across all films in those markets would require a different comparison. The makers have yet to publish individual gross figures for all 27 markets, so the full ranking cannot be independently checked from the available breakdown. [Moneycontrol’s coverage of the announcement](https://www.moneycontrol.com/entertainment/ranbir-kapoor-s-film-becomes-highest-grossing-indian-production-in-27-countries-collects-rs-1800-crore-worldwide-article-14090302.html)

## Two milestones to watch in week four

The film entered week four just **₹7.90 crore short of ₹1,000 crore India net**. Day 22 brought in **₹9.65 crore net** in India, compared with **₹64.85 crore** on opening day. Thursday’s number shows how much the daily pace has cooled since release. The first complete weekend of week four will show whether ticket sales pick up again. Both round-number milestones here use the same Day 22 cutoff. [Sacnilk’s day-by-day figures](https://www.sacnilk.com/news/ramayana_part_1_2026_Box_Office_Collection_Day_Wise_Worldwide)

## Cast, release and Part 2

Nitesh Tiwari directed the film, which opened on **5 November 2026** in Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada and Malayalam. **Ranbir Kapoor** plays Rama, **Sai Pallavi** plays Sita and **Yash** plays Ravana. **Sunny Deol** plays Hanuman and **Ravie Dubey** plays Lakshman. The official trailer credits **A. R. Rahman and Hans Zimmer** for the music, **DNEG** for visual effects and **Sony Pictures Entertainment** for global distribution. Moneycontrol reports that the second part is planned for **2028**.
