The final **Avengers: Doomsday** trailer drew a reported **563 million views in its first 24 hours**, the highest first-day figure reported for a Disney film trailer. Sony's first **Spider-Man: Brand New Day** trailer still leads the reported overall ranking with **718.6 million views**. The gap is **155.6 million views**, or **15.56 crore**.

The Doomsday figure comes from WaveMetrix data reported by Deadline and covered by [Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/2026/11/25/avengers-doomsday-final-trailer-breaks-records-to-become-second-most-viewed-of-all-time). [ET Now](https://www.etnownews.com/entertainment/avengers-doomsday-final-trailer-sets-disney-record-with-563-million-views-in-24-hours-becomes-second-most-viewed-movie-trailer-ever-article-156565207) says Disney has yet to confirm the figure independently.

## How the first 24 hours compare

| Trailer or promo | Reported views |
| --- | ---: |
| Spider-Man: Brand New Day — first official trailer | 718.6 million (71.86 crore) |
| Avengers: Doomsday — final trailer | 563 million (56.3 crore) |
| Avengers: Doomsday — earlier promo | 503 million (50.3 crore) |

[Variety India](https://varietyindia.com/avengers-doomsday-final-trailer-becomes-disneys-most-viewed-ever-from-spider-man-brand-new-day-to-deadpool-wolverine-all-24-hou/) reports the three figures. Doomsday added **60 million first-day views** compared with its earlier promo, an increase of about **12%**. Its final trailer reached roughly **78%** of Spider-Man's first-trailer tally.

## Why the Disney record and the overall ranking differ

The two trailers feature Marvel characters, but the releases sit in different studio portfolios. Marvel Studios' Doomsday is a Disney release. Sony releases Brand New Day. That puts Doomsday at the top of the reported Disney ranking while Sony's Spider-Man leads the wider comparison. The ranking also compares two different campaign stages: Doomsday's final trailer and Brand New Day's first official trailer.

## What the view count measures

The reported total combines views across platforms during the first day. The counter under a single YouTube upload reflects that upload alone. Replays and views across multiple platforms can also mean one viewer contributes more than one view. The total describes how widely the trailer circulated. Box-office performance will be measured separately once the film opens.

## Avengers: Doomsday release date in India

The film is scheduled to reach Indian cinemas on **December 17, 2026**, according to [Variety India's release report](https://varietyindia.com/avengers-doomsday-to-release-in-india-on-december-17-a-day-before-us-robert-downey-jr-and-chris-evans-share-diwali-wishes/). [Disney's official film guide](https://www.disneyplus.com/explore/articles/avengers-doomsday) lists **December 18, 2026** for the U.S. release and hosts the trailer. Disney also confirms that Robert Downey Jr. plays Victor von Doom and that the film brings together the Avengers, Fantastic Four, X-Men and Wakandans.
