# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 20, 2026**

At TechWadh (https://techwadh.in/), we respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you visit our website and how it may be used.

TechWadh does not require visitors to create an account to read our articles.

When you contact us by email, we may receive your email address and any information you choose to include in your message.

Our website hosting or security providers may automatically process technical information such as IP addresses, browser details and request logs to operate and protect the website.

## 2. Cookies and Similar Technologies

TechWadh or its service providers may use essential cookies or similar technologies for website functionality, security and performance.

If analytics or advertising services are introduced, they may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own privacy policies. We will update this policy as our services change.

## 3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

- Respond to questions and feedback.
- Maintain website security and functionality.
- Understand and improve website performance where analytics are enabled.
- Meet applicable legal requirements.

We do not sell visitors' personal information.

## 4. Third-Party Services and Links

Our articles may link to external websites, manufacturers, retailers and other services. These websites operate under their own privacy policies. TechWadh is not responsible for their privacy practices.

## 5. Affiliate Links and Advertising

Some future articles may contain affiliate links. If you make a qualifying purchase through such a link, TechWadh may earn a commission at no additional cost to you.

If advertising services are introduced, third-party advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver or measure advertisements. Relevant disclosures will be provided where applicable.

## 6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled through our website. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 7. Children's Privacy

TechWadh is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children through account registration.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, services or legal obligations change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised date.
