AC Tatkal is about to open and both apps are on your phone. **Start with the app you’ve already set up and know how to use.** Railway releases do not document a separate Tatkal seat pool or booking priority for RailOne. If your train starts its run a day before you board, double-check the opening date before you choose an app. [Railways on the reservation system](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2283380&lang=2&reg=48) · [RailOne features](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2298355&lang=1&reg=48)

## RailOne or IRCTC Rail Connect: which should you open?

| If you want… | Use… |
|---|---|
| Reserved booking alongside platform and unreserved tickets, PNR enquiries and RailMadad | RailOne |
| A reserved-booking flow you already know and have set up | IRCTC Rail Connect |
| A separate supply of Tatkal berths | Neither. Choose based on your familiarity with the checkout. |

A seat count can change between looking it up and submitting the booking. If two screens disagree, first check that both searches use the same route segment and class. They may also have refreshed at different moments. A faster-looking screen alone is not evidence of an allocation advantage.

## The date mistake that costs you a day

IRCTC says Tatkal opens **one day before the train leaves its originating station**, excluding that departure date. For eligible trains and classes, **AC opens at 10:00 a.m. IST** and **non-AC at 11:00 a.m. IST**.

Suppose your train starts its run on Friday but reaches your boarding station on Saturday. Its Tatkal window opens **Thursday**. You still select Saturday as your journey date when searching from your boarding station. Check that the result is for the intended train run. [IRCTC Tatkal FAQ](https://contents.irctc.co.in/en/TatkalFaq.html)

## Five checks before the window opens

1. **Check your IRCTC profile early.** IRCTC Rail Connect requires an Aadhaar-authenticated account for online Tatkal. Keep the Aadhaar-linked phone available and complete any OTP prompt shown during booking. Railways described the booking-time OTP rollout as phased. [IRCTC’s app guide](https://contents.irctc.co.in/en/MobileAppUserGuideAadhaarAuthenticationForTatkalBooking.pdf) · [Railways’ OTP update](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2202197)
2. **Keep a backup train or class in mind.** Check the route and boarding station. Verify whether the class actually offers Tatkal.
3. **Review passenger details.** Have names and ages handy. Use saved details if the booking screen offers them.
4. **Pick a payment method you already use.** Have its PIN or verification method ready. Review the actual fare and ticket status in the app.
5. **Confirm the booking in your history.** Look for a PNR and read whether it is confirmed, RAC or waitlisted before treating the trip as booked.

## Money debited, no ticket: what now?

Check booked tickets and transaction history on the channel you used before paying again. A debit can happen even when a booking fails. Keep the transaction reference and check its status through the app’s support or your bank. The failed-payment process depends on whether the payment reached IRCTC. [IRCTC’s failed-payment guide](https://contents.irctc.co.in/en/Alerts_mone_debited.pdf)

## Can you travel on a waitlisted Tatkal e-ticket?

If **all passengers on the e-ticket are still waitlisted after chart preparation**, IRCTC says their names are dropped from the chart and they cannot travel on that e-ticket. A confirmed Tatkal ticket also generally carries **no refund for voluntary cancellation**, with separate rules for specified contingencies. Check the status and applicable terms before cancelling or boarding. [Waitlisted e-ticket rules](https://contents.irctc.co.in/en/Waitlisted_E-Ticket.html) · [Refund terms](https://contents.irctc.co.in/en/Terms%20and%20conditions.pdf)
