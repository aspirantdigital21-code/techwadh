## Educational Qualification

The prescribed minimum qualification for Station Master is:

- A **Graduate Degree from a recognised university or its equivalent**.
- The required qualification must be held by the closing date of the online application, **25 November 2026**.
- Candidates whose final results for the prescribed qualification are still awaited after the closing date are not eligible under the notification.

Keep your degree certificate or provisional certificate and other supporting documents ready for verification.

## How to Apply Online

Applications are accepted online through the official RRB application portal.

1. Visit [https://www.rrbapply.gov.in/](https://www.rrbapply.gov.in/).
2. Create an account or sign in using your existing RRB credentials.
3. Read the CEN 08/2026 instructions and check your eligibility.
4. Select your preferred RRB carefully. The chosen RRB cannot be changed later.

6. Upload the photograph, signature and documents in the specified format.
