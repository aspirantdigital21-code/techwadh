**Jennifer Barreto of Puerto Rico won Miss Universe 2026.** What she will receive for winning is less clear. Media reports have cited a cash prize of about **$250,000** and an allowance of about **$50,000 per month**. As of November 25, 2026, neither figure had been officially confirmed.

## What do the prize reports say?

| Claim | What we know |
|---|---|
| Jennifer Barreto won Miss Universe 2026 | Confirmed by Telemundo |
| About $250,000 in prize money | Reported estimate |
| About $50,000 in monthly allowance | Reported estimate |

The [Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/entertainment/miss-universe-2026-winner-250000-plus-monthly-allowance-heres-how-much-prize-money-jennifer-barreto-of-puerto-rico-will-reportedly-get) describes both figures as unconfirmed. The [Financial Express](https://www.financialexpress.com/life/lifestyle/miss-universe-2026-economics-how-puerto-ricos-8-7-million-bet-new-ownership-rules-are-reshaping-the-pageant/4393524/) reports that the financial terms have not been made public. Without those terms, the two figures cannot establish a confirmed total payout.

## Why the win mattered for Puerto Rico

The 75th edition took place in San Juan, Puerto Rico, on November 24 local time. Mexico’s Fátima Bosch, the 2025 titleholder, crowned Barreto at the final. The win gave Puerto Rico its **sixth Miss Universe title** and its first since 2006.

[Telemundo’s winner announcement](https://nbcuniversalnewsgroup.com/telemundo/2026/11/25/jennifer-barreto-of-puerto-rico-is-crowned-miss-universe-2026-during-the-historic-75th-edition-broadcast-live-on-telemundo-from-puerto-rico/) · [Miss Universe Puerto Rico’s report](https://muniversepr.com/noticias/llega-la-sexta-corona-para-puerto-rico-jennifer-barreto-es-miss-universe-2026/)

## Who is Jennifer Barreto?

Barreto, 27, represented San Sebastián at the Puerto Rico pageant. Winning the local title earned her the chance to represent Puerto Rico at the global final, which was held on home soil.

Outside pageantry, her background is in psychology and social work. She studied psychology as an undergraduate in Kentucky and went on to earn master’s degrees in psychology and social work. Coverage of the final also identified her as the first mother to win the Miss Universe title. [GMA News profile](https://www.gmanetwork.com/news/lifestyle/content/1010736/who-is-miss-universe-2026-jennifer-barreto/story/) · [Pageant coverage](https://www.untvnews.com/news/puerto-ricos-jennifer-barreto-crowned-miss-universe-2026-makes-history-as-first-mother-to-win-title)

## Inside the final and the question about AI

Telemundo reported that **116 contestants** took part in the 75th edition. Melissa Núñez of the Dominican Republic finished as first runner-up, followed by Venezuela’s Clara Vegas as second runner-up. For the host audience in San Juan, Barreto’s win ended Puerto Rico’s wait for a title since 2006. [Final results](https://www.untvnews.com/news/puerto-ricos-jennifer-barreto-crowned-miss-universe-2026-makes-history-as-first-mother-to-win-title)

One question put to Barreto asked what human value should be preserved as AI becomes more widespread. Her answer emphasized using AI carefully and taking responsibility for the values that guide its use.
