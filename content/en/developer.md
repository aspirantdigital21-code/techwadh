**Solo developer of TechWadh**  
B.Sc. IT · University of Mumbai

Rohit Sarem Pasi develops and independently manages TechWadh. He handles the site from design and implementation through deployment and ongoing maintenance.

## What he works on

- **Design and front-end development.** Builds the layouts, navigation and article pages, with particular attention to readability and usability on phones and desktops.
- **Hindi and English publishing.** Maintains the page templates, categories, archives and search so readers can find articles in their preferred language.
- **Build and deployment.** Uses Hugo to generate the site and GitHub to track changes. Checks builds, links and page behavior before publishing updates.
- **Maintenance.** Fixes issues that surface on the live site and improves the publishing workflow as TechWadh grows.

His approach is to keep the site easy to read, straightforward to navigate and practical for one person to maintain.
